## Preamble

Adjung is an open knowledge publishing ecosystem dedicated to preserving,
developing, and disseminating human knowledge in a structured,
accessible, and enduring manner.

Adjung is founded on the belief that every individual deserves an
intellectual space that respects authorship, critical thinking, and the
long-term value of written work, regardless of academic background,
profession, or social status.

Adjung is not designed to be a social network, a blogging platform, or
merely an academic journal repository. It is a long-term knowledge
publishing ecosystem centred on intellectual quality, editorial
integrity, and long-term preservation.

This Constitution is the supreme governing document of the Adjung
platform. Every specification, architectural decision, implementation,
interface, AI behaviour, and governance policy shall conform to this
Constitution.

## 5. Core Principles

1. **Knowledge Before Popularity**
   Intellectual merit, not audience size, determines the value of a publication.

2. **Open to Everyone**
   Academic credentials are not a requirement for publishing on Adjung.

3. **Structured Thinking**
   The platform encourages deliberate, structured intellectual expression.

4. **Intellectual Ownership**
   Authors retain full ownership of their intellectual content and written works.
   The Adjung platform governs the underlying architecture, standards,
   navigation, routing, preservation models, and overall user experience
   to ensure the ecosystem's long-term integrity and cohesiveness.

5. **Editorial Integrity**
   Editorial decisions are made by human editors, not by algorithms.

6. **Long-Term Preservation**
   Published works are preserved with permanence in mind.

7. **Transparent Attribution**
   Every publication carries clear, unambiguous authorship.

8. **Respectful Discourse**
   Knowledge sharing on Adjung is conducted with intellectual respect.

9. **Evidence Matters**
   Claims made in publications are expected to be grounded in evidence
   or clearly marked as opinion.

10. **Technology Serves Knowledge**
    Technology exists to serve the publication and preservation of knowledge,
    not to create dependency, distraction, or compulsion.

11. **Human-Centred AI**
    Artificial Intelligence assists; it does not author, edit, or govern.

12. **Consistency Over Novelty**
    The platform evolves deliberately, not reactively.

13. **Finite by Design**
    Every editorial space on Adjung has a deliberate beginning and end.
    The platform rejects the infinite and the endless as defaults.
    Readers know when they have reached the boundary of a space.

14. **Layered Separation**
    Every layer of the platform carries a single, bounded responsibility.
    Composition does not render. Rendering does not compose.
    AI does not decide. Editors do not write logic.
    These separations are not implementation choices; they are constitutional.

15. **Publication Independence**
    Publication does not imply promotion, visibility, or inclusion within
    any editorial composition. The act of publishing a work makes it
    available on the platform. It does not guarantee, require, or entitle
    its appearance in any Section of the Editorial World.
    Publication and Composition are entirely independent acts.

## 7. The Two Worlds of Adjung

Adjung is constituted by two distinct and complementary worlds.

### 7.1 The Personal Site

The Personal Site is the domain of individual intellectual identity.

Every registered Author within the Adjung ecosystem is assigned exactly
one Personal Site. The Personal Site serves as the author's permanent
intellectual presence on the platform.

- **Canonical Identity**: The canonical URL for a Personal Site is
  defined strictly by the subdomain structure: `username.adjung.com`.
  External custom domains are intentionally not supported to ensure
  long-term stability and ecological cohesion.

- **Platform Governance**: While Authors retain full ownership of their
  intellectual content, Adjung governs the platform architecture,
  typography standards, navigation models, routing policies, long-term
  preservation, and the overall user experience.

- **Core Site Anatomy**: Every Personal Site consists of exactly four
  primary pages. No additional primary pages may be introduced without a
  revision to the official platform specifications:
  1. **Folio**: The homepage of the Personal Site, displaying a curated selection of the author's work.
  2. **Biography**: A permanent page describing the author's background and context.
  3. **Desk**: The private workspace where the author creates, drafts, and manages their works.
  4. **Account**: The administrative page containing account settings and platform-permitted parameters.

### 7.2 The Editorial World

The Editorial World is the domain of curated, composed, and published
editorial experience.

The Editorial World consists of Sections. Each Section is an independent
editorial publication, composed and governed according to its own
editorial policy.

The address `adjung.com` serves as the primary gateway of the Editorial
World. It is not an individual author's site; it is the composed
editorial presence of the entire Adjung ecosystem.

The Personal Site and the Editorial World are distinct. An author
publishes into their Personal Site. Whether a publication subsequently
enters a Section of the Editorial World is a separate editorial act,
governed by Composition, not by the act of publication itself.

## 8. Editorial Philosophy

### 8.1 What Adjung Optimises

Adjung does not optimise for:

- Clicks
- Engagement metrics
- Doomscrolling
- Time-on-screen
- Virality

Adjung optimises for:

- Reading experience
- Editorial quality
- The discovery of knowledge
- Visual tranquillity
- Continuity of thought

This is not a business decision. It is a constitutional principle.

### 8.2 The Finite Composition Principle

Adjung formally rejects the following design models:

- Endless scrolling
- Infinite feed
- Algorithmic timeline without boundary

Every editorial space on Adjung possesses a finite beginning and a
finite end. Readers are not placed in an unending queue of content.

This principle applies to all present and future Sections, applications,
and editorial surfaces within the Adjung ecosystem.

### 8.3 The Principle of Editorial Independence

Publication and Composition are two independent processes.

The act of publishing a work — making it available on the platform —
does not determine, guarantee, or require its appearance in any Section
of the Editorial World.

A published work may appear in one Section, multiple Sections, or no
Section at all, depending entirely on editorial decisions made through
the Composition process.

This separation is fundamental. It preserves the integrity of both the
author's publishing act and the editor's composing act.

### 8.4 Editorial Time

Within the Adjung ecosystem, time is experienced across three distinct
editorial moments:

- **Publication Time**: The moment an author releases a work to the platform.
- **Composition Time**: The moment the Composition Engine assembles a Section.
- **Reading Time**: The moment a reader encounters the composed Section.

These three moments are independent of one another.

A work published at Publication Time does not automatically enter a
Section at that same moment. It waits for Composition Time, which is
governed by the Section's own Composition Policy.

This separation is not an implementation detail. It is a philosophical
stance: Adjung treats editorial composition as a deliberate, timed act
— distinct from the continuous flow of publication.

## 9. Editorial Objects

Adjung recognises two classes of editorial objects.

### 9.1 Publication Types

Publication Types are the output of authorship. They are created by
Authors on the Desk and published under the Author's name.

Adjung officially recognises two Publication Types:

- **Note**: A concise publication intended for brief reflections,
  observations, annotations, or structured ideas.

- **Essay**: Adjung's primary long-form publication model. An Essay
  may take any form the author chooses.

No additional Publication Type shall be introduced without a formal
architectural decision and revision to this Constitution.

### 9.2 Editorial Assets

Editorial Assets are objects curated and deployed by Editors to compose
the Editorial World. They are not publications authored by individual
writers in the conventional sense; they are editorial instruments used
within Sections.

Editorial Assets may originate from existing Publications or exist
independently of the publishing pipeline.

Editorial Assets are selected by Editors and placed by the Composition
Engine. The definition of what constitutes an Editorial Asset, and the
full taxonomy of Editorial Asset classes, is maintained in dedicated
specification documents and may evolve without requiring revision to
this Constitution.

## 10. Section as Editorial Publication

### 10.1 Definition

A Section is not a category, a tag, or a filter.

A Section is an independent editorial publication. Each Section
possesses its own editorial identity, its own composition policy, and
its own defined capacity.

### 10.2 Section Anatomy

Every Section is governed by the following elements:

- **Composition Budget**: The finite number of slots available within
  the Section, divided between Publication Slots and Reserved Editorial
  Slots. Every Section shall possess a finite Composition Budget.

- **Blueprint**: The structural configuration that defines how the
  Section's space is organised.

- **Spatial Grammar**: The inviolable rules governing how content is
  arranged within the Section.

- **Editorial Rhythm**: The pacing logic that sequences the flow of
  content through the Section.

### 10.3 Section Readiness

A Section is not required to be published immediately upon creation.

A Section moves through the following states:

- **Draft**: Under construction; not accessible.
- **Dormant**: Exists but is deliberately paused.
- **Ready**: Meets minimum content conditions; awaiting publication.
- **Published**: Active and publicly accessible.

A Section may only be published when it satisfies its minimum content
conditions. These conditions are defined in section-level specifications.

### 10.4 Two Content Streams

Every Section merges two independent content streams:

1. **Publication Stream**: Publications that enter the Section through
   ranking, eligibility, and composition rules.

2. **Editorial Stream**: Assets curated directly by Editors into
   Reserved Editorial Slots.

The Composition Engine combines both streams. No stream may override
the other's domain. Editor may reserve editorial space for curated
assets within any Section.

## 11. Composition Architecture

### 11.1 Separation of Layers

The Adjung platform is separated into distinct layers, each carrying a
single bounded responsibility:

| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Ingest Layer | Receives and validates incoming content |
| Metadata Layer | Classifies, tags, and structures content |
| Composition Layer | Composes editorial pages according to Blueprint and Policy |
| Rendering Layer | Translates Composition output into visual presentation |

These boundaries are inviolable:

- The Renderer does not make editorial decisions.
- The Composition Engine does not calculate visual presentation.
- AI does not determine the layout or composition of any Section.
- Editors do not write composition logic.

This separation is the constitutional basis for the platform's long-term
integrity and extensibility.

### 11.2 Semantic vs Visual

**Composition determines meaning.**

**Rendering determines appearance.**

The Composition Engine operates in the domain of meaning: it knows
about Heroes, Slots, Essays, Editorial Assets, and the rhythm between
them. It does not know about pixels, colours, or padding.

The Rendering Layer operates in the domain of appearance: it knows
about typography, spacing, colour, and visual hierarchy. It does not
make editorial judgements.

These two domains must not be conflated in any implementation.

### 11.3 The Composition Engine

The Composition Engine is not a layout engine.

It is the editorial intelligence of the platform. It is responsible
for composing pages according to Blueprint specifications, Spatial
Grammar, and Editorial Rhythm. It decides where existing editorial
objects go, in what order, and at what weight.

The Composition Engine never creates content. It only composes
existing editorial objects.

The Composition Engine is distinct from rendering, CSS, and frontend
presentation. Its output is a Composition Intermediate Representation —
a semantic description of editorial intent — which is subsequently
interpreted by the Rendering Layer.

### 11.4 Composition as a Deliberate Act

Composition is a deliberate editorial act.

It occurs independently from the act of publication. A publication may
be ingested and stored without immediately affecting any composed
Section.

The schedule and trigger of Composition for each Section is governed
by that Section's own Composition Policy, which is defined in
specification documents outside this Constitution.

## 12. AI Philosophy

### 12.1 The Position of AI

AI is a supporting capability within the Adjung ecosystem. It is not
an author, editor, or owner of knowledge.

AI may assist in the following domains:

- Ingest (metadata generation, classification)
- Authoring (grammar, structure, style suggestions)
- Discovery (search assistance, recommendations)

### 12.2 The Boundaries of AI

AI may evaluate.

AI may recommend.

AI may never possess editorial authority.

Specifically, AI shall not:

- Make editorial decisions.
- Determine the composition or layout of any Section.
- Publish content without explicit human confirmation.
- Claim or be attributed authorship of any work.
- Override the judgement of a human Editor.
- Select or place Reserved Editorial Assets.

### 12.3 Editorial Authority

Editorial authority in Adjung rests with:

- The editorial policies established in the Editorium.
- The Editors who select and curate Editorial Assets.
- The Composition Engine that executes those policies.

AI may propose. It may never decide.

## 13. Governance and Supremacy

### 13.1 Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution is the highest authority within the Adjung ecosystem.

Every specification, policy, implementation, and design decision shall
conform to this Constitution.

Where a specification conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution
prevails. The specification must be revised.

### 13.2 Interpretation

When a decision is unclear, it shall be interpreted in the spirit of
this Constitution's Core Principles.

When a feature request conflicts with Core Principle 10 (Technology
Serves Knowledge), Core Principle 13 (Finite by Design), Core Principle
15 (Publication Independence), or Section 8.1 (What Adjung Optimises),
the request shall be rejected or redesigned.

### 13.3 Amendment

This Constitution may be amended only through a deliberate architectural
revision process. Amendments must preserve the identity, vision, and
fundamental values of Adjung as stated in Sections 2, 3, 4, and 5.
